I'm a researcher in the behavioural sciences with a passion for research methods, data science, and statistics. In particular, I am interested in the biological and cultural evolution of social behaviour in humans.

I received my PhD from the University of Auckland in 2022. I now work as a Senior Research Associate at the University of Bristol. My work has been published in top academic journals, including Nature Communications, Nature Human Behaviour, PNAS, Proceedings of the Royal Society B, and Scientific Reports.
